The Problem

Great artisans. Limited global access.

India has millions of talented artisans creating products with generations of knowledge behind them. Yet many remain disconnected from international markets.

CraftBridge is changing that. We are building an AI-powered sourcing platform that connects Indian artisans directly with global buyers — while simplifying the complex processes between creation and commerce.

Artisans, products, procurement, AI, warehousing, export and international sales — one connected ecosystem.
