Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

CraftBridge (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates craftbridgeworld.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We aim to collect as little as possible.

Who we are

CraftBridge is based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. If you have any question about your privacy or this policy, email us at hello@craftbridgeworld.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. If you contact us by email or send an inquiry through a form on our site, we receive the details you choose to share — such as your name, business name, email address, phone number, your role, and your message. We use this only to respond to you.
  • Usage and analytics data. When you visit, our analytics provider may collect standard technical information — such as pages viewed, approximate location (derived from IP address, not precise), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. This is used in aggregate to understand and improve the site, and it runs only after you accept cookies (see below).
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider may automatically log basic request data (such as IP address and timestamp) to operate, deliver, and secure the site.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand site traffic, with Google Consent Mode v2. Analytics storage is denied by default — no analytics cookies are placed on your device until you click “Accept” on our cookie banner. If you decline, Google may still receive anonymous, cookieless signals but does not store cookies on your device.

After you accept, cookies such as _ga and _ga_* (Google Analytics) may be set to distinguish visitors and sessions. You can change your choice at any time using the “Cookie Preferences” link in our footer.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide the information or services you ask about.
  • To understand and improve our website and offerings.
  • To keep the site secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing and third parties

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site, and only as needed:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) — website analytics. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — website hosting and content delivery.
  • Hostinger — our domain and email provider.

These providers process data on our behalf, and some may process it outside your country.

International transfers

We are based in India and use service providers that may store or process data in other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

Data retention

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable follow-up, then delete it. Analytics data is retained according to our analytics provider’s settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or India’s DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email hello@craftbridgeworld.com. You can withdraw analytics consent at any time using “Cookie Preferences”.

Children

Our site is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email hello@craftbridgeworld.com — CraftBridge, Jaipur, Rajasthan, India.
